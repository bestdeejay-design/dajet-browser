# Dajet — visual identity pack

**Selected direction: Datum.** A custom D-shaped aperture: a clear, quiet signature that stays recognizable at 16 px without borrowing the upstream S mark.

Dajet is pronounced **dah-JET**. The name joins *da* ("yes" in Russian) with *jet* (speed). The design keeps the browser's chrome quiet and lets the page take the lead.

## Voice

- Primary: **Silence, styled.**
- Alternate: **The web, at ease.**
- Alternate: **A quieter way through.**

## Assets

- [`mark.svg`](mark.svg) — single even-odd outline on a 100 × 100 transparent canvas.
- [`mark-path.txt`](mark-path.txt) — paste-ready absolute path and canvas size.
- [`palette.json`](palette.json) — copy-ready light/dark ink and plate colors, plus the brand accent.
- [`iconset/`](iconset/) — ten PNGs: five nominal sizes and their 2× exports. Snow-white plate, 22.37% corner radius, centered 75%-width mark, soft shadow.
- [`wallpapers/`](wallpapers/) — 5120 × 2880 light and dark JPEG compositions. The dark field is `#171717`; the two layouts are deliberately composed differently, not color-inverted.
- [`theme-default/`](theme-default/) — manifest, San Francisco system-font stack, light/dark theme variables, and start-screen clock/link-grid styling.
- [`landing/hero.png`](landing/hero.png) — 2560 × 1440 browser-window mockup.
- [`landing/og-image.png`](landing/og-image.png) — 1200 × 630 social preview.

The landing visuals are illustrative launch mockups, not captures of the current application. The assets are flat and self-contained; no external fonts or runtime code are required.

## Contrast

The palette's light ink/plate pair is **17.46:1**; the dark pair is **15.77:1**. The light theme uses the deep-rust accent `#7A3B26`; in dark mode it switches to the lighter copper `#E8AE8C` so accent text and focus states remain high contrast.
